Chapter 1 · Culminating Activity

Mission 01: The Network Simulation Readiness Audit

You are a newly assigned network laboratory assistant. Before the class begins building networks, the laboratory coordinator needs proof that Cisco Packet Tracer is installed, usable, and that you can locate and inspect the basic tools needed for future exercises. Your assignment is to perform a short readiness audit and submit evidence of your work.

Estimated duration
35–50 minutes
Work mode
Individual · Hands-on
Deliverables
.pkt file + evidence report
01 · Mission Brief

What you will demonstrate

Learning targets

Launch Packet Tracer, identify the Logical workspace and device palette, open device windows, explore PC configuration tools and switch CLI, and save a Packet Tracer project.

Success criteria

You can locate required controls without a full walkthrough, explain what you observed, and provide evidence that your saved project opens correctly.

What you need

  • Cisco Packet Tracer installed and signed in, where required.
  • A computer capable of running the application and a folder for your submission.
  • Optional: the built-in sample dhcp_reservation under File → Open Samples → 01 Networking → DHCP, if present in your version.
  • A screenshot tool to capture evidence. No downloadable starter topology is required.

Chapter 1 covers installation and exploring Packet Tracer. This is an original instructor-designed assessment, not a reproduction of a publisher-provided lab. The small topology below is an interface-familiarization task; IP addressing and end-to-end connectivity are assessed in Chapter 2.
