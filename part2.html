Part II · Culminating Activity

Mission 02: Campus Network Commissioning Challenge

Mapúa MCL is preparing a small instructional laboratory and an adjacent faculty office. As the junior network technician, you have been assigned to design, build, configure, and verify a two-LAN wired network in Cisco Packet Tracer, then demonstrate a basic wireless extension. Your supervisor expects clear addressing, functioning connectivity, documented evidence, and a troubleshooting record—not just a screenshot of connected devices.

Estimated duration
120–180 minutes
Work mode
Individual · Practical assessment
Deliverables
.pkt + evidence report + screenshots
01 · Mission Brief

Scope and learning outcomes

This original capstone integrates the skills and concepts introduced in Part II of the supplied Cisco Press book. The wireless task is a guided observation of wireless principles; VLANs, OSPF, NAT, ACLs, and enterprise wireless controllers are intentionally outside this assessment.

Chapter 4 · Cisco IOS Basics

Navigate IOS modes, configure hostnames and interface addresses, use show commands, and save configurations.

Chapter 5 · Physical Interfaces and Cabling Types

Select appropriate Ethernet cables, identify interface status, and diagnose physical connectivity.

Chapter 6 · IPv4 Addressing and Subnetting

Apply /24 IPv4 addressing, subnet masks, default gateways, and verify end-to-end reachability.

Chapter 7 · IPv6

Configure IPv6 global unicast addresses, enable IPv6 routing, and verify IPv6 connectivity.

Chapter 8 · Wireless Principles

Identify SSID, wireless association, and coverage/security considerations using a simple wireless device.

Chapter 9 · Switching Basics

Observe MAC address learning and verify the switch forwarding table.

Required resources

  • Cisco Packet Tracer 8.2.1 or later (or a version supporting the devices and commands used).
  • One Cisco 2911 router, two Cisco 2960 switches, two PCs, one server, one wireless access point, and one wireless laptop.
  • Ethernet copper straight-through cables; compatible wireless adapter on the laptop if required.
  • A folder to save the Packet Tracer project and screenshots. Device port names can vary by model.

04 · Verification Toolkit

Commands and expected results

Router interface health
show ip interface brief
show ipv6 interface brief

R1 G0/0 and G0/1 should be up/up with their assigned addresses.

Configuration persistence
show running-config
show startup-config

Confirm hostname, interfaces, and saved startup configuration.

Switch learning
show mac address-table

After generating traffic, learned dynamic MAC addresses should appear on relevant ports.

End-device testing
ipconfig
ping 192.168.20.10
ping 2001:DB8:20::10

Confirm IP parameters and connectivity; initial packets may time out during address resolution.

Troubleshooting order: physical link → interface up/down → address/mask → gateway → IPv6 routing → wireless association. Do not introduce static routes: both LANs are directly connected to R1.
