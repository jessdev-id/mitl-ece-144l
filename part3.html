Mission 03 · Culminating Activity

Campus Network Access and Resiliency Challenge

A new academic building requires separate Engineering and Administration access networks, resilient interswitch connectivity and a documented wireless expansion plan. As the assigned network engineer, you must commission the switching infrastructure, validate VLAN separation, inspect neighbor discovery and spanning-tree behavior, and prove that the network tolerates an individual redundant-link failure.

Estimated duration
150–210 minutes
Work mode
Individual · Hands-on
Deliverables

01 · Mission Brief

Scope and success criteria

Learning targets

Ch. 10: VLANs, trunks and EtherChannel; Ch. 11: CDP and LLDP; Ch. 12: Rapid PVST+ and redundant paths; Ch. 13: wireless architecture and AP modes.

Successful outcome

Same-VLAN hosts communicate across switches, different VLANs remain isolated without routing, LACP forms a port-channel, STP prevents loops, neighbor tables are correct and the network reconverges after a controlled link failure.

Required equipment

Cisco Packet Tracer; 3 Catalyst 2960 switches (SW1, SW2, SW3); 4 PCs (PC-A through PC-D); copper Ethernet cables; optional AP-PT for architecture inspection. Use the CLI for switch configuration. This is an original instructor-designed assessment based on the book's Part III topics.

02 · Topology and Addressing

Required logical topology

Logical reference · not a live Packet Tracer workspace

The two SW1–SW2 links are members of one logical EtherChannel. The paths through SW3 are independent trunks; Rapid PVST+ must manage the resulting redundant topology.

DeviceAccess portVLANIPv4 address / mask
PC-A (SW1)Fa0/110192.168.10.11 / 255.255.255.0
PC-B (SW1)Fa0/220192.168.20.11 / 255.255.255.0
PC-C (SW2)Fa0/110192.168.10.12 / 255.255.255.0
PC-D (SW2)Fa0/220192.168.20.12 / 255.255.255.0

No router or Layer 3 SVI is required. A failed cross-VLAN ping is expected and is not evidence of a fault in this assessment.

05 · Fault Injection

Controlled troubleshooting challenge

Fault A — Incorrect VLAN assignment

Temporarily move SW2 Fa0/1 to VLAN 20, then repeat PC-A → PC-C ping. Diagnose using show vlan brief and correct the port membership. Record the failed and recovered results.

Fault B — Redundant trunk failure

Temporarily shut down SW1 Fa0/23 (the SW1–SW3 trunk). Verify remaining paths and recovery after STP reconvergence. Restore with no shutdown. Do not shut down the EtherChannel unless instructed.

Faults are deliberate and must be reversed before saving the final submission. Document Layer 1/2 evidence first, then test Layer 3 reachability.
