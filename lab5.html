Physical Media

Copper UTP

Electrical signaling over twisted pairs. The book highlights Cat 5e for 1 Gbps and Cat 6 for 10 Gbps.

Multimode Fiber

Optical signaling with multiple propagation modes through a fiber core.

Single-mode Fiber

A smaller fiber core and laser transmission along a single mode, suitable for long-distance links.

Fiber installations must respect the cable's bend radius. The medium and optics must match the intended link.

Traditional cabling uses straight-through connections for unlike devices and crossover for like devices. Auto MDI-X makes the distinction less important on supported ports.

How Links Operate

Full- vs. Half-Duplex

Switched point-to-point links can send and receive simultaneously in full-duplex mode. Legacy shared media and hubs use half-duplex with CSMA/CD.

Power over Ethernet

PoE supplies power over network cabling for phones, wireless access points, and IP cameras. The book describes 802.3af, 802.3at, and 802.3bt.

Serial Connections

Legacy serial links may involve DTE and DCE ends. The command show controllers helps identify cable information such as DCE and clock rate.

Troubleshooting with show interface

Use show interface gi0/1 to examine interface state, negotiated speed and duplex, traffic, and error counters.

CRC / input errors

May point to damaged cabling, interference, or framing problems.

Collisions / late collisions

Unexpected on healthy full-duplex links; investigate duplex mismatch.

Runts

Frames shorter than 64 bytes.

Giants / jumbo frames

Frames larger than standard Ethernet framing limits; inspect size and configuration.
